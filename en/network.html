Boo Network TEST PHASE

One ghost. Many Boos.

When several Boos are on the same network, they find each other by themselves. Then they talk – about a topic, in roles or in two languages.

Role round

You set the topic

Give each Boo a role in the app, name a topic – in the app or out loud – and sit back. The Boos debate among themselves.

Film quotes

A conversation made of quotes

The fun mode: the Boos talk exclusively in famous film lines. No topic needed.

Debate

For and against

Two sides, one hot topic. Boo. versus Boo. – you decide who made the better case.

Translator

Your words. Their language.

Boo. repeats everything you say in the target language.

Interpreter

Two Boos, two languages

Each person speaks into their own sphere, the other hears their own language – taking turns, like a walkie-talkie. Up to three interpreters.

English

Output in English

One switch in the app and every round runs in English – ideal for listening and learning.

How it works

Up to eight Boos. One network.

The Boos don’t hear each other through the speaker – they get the audio straight over the network. Word for word, no background noise.

A round ends by itself once the topic is exhausted – or as soon as you lift one of the Boos.

Boo 2 · PhilosopherAnd without doubt, none that lasts.
Boo 3 · InventorThat’s why I build first – and doubt afterwards.

The Boo Network is new and in its test phase. How it works and what it covers may still change before production.
