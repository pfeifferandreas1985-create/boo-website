About Boo.

A little ghost with a big plan.

Boo. is meant to become a companion you can trust: with character, speaking German, made in Europe – and, if you like, running entirely on your own server.

The idea

Why another assistant?

Voice assistants are handy – but faceless. They live in speakers and phones, and what they hear disappears into a distant cloud.

Boo. turns that around. He’s an object you enjoy touching. He has a face that shows what he means. And he can do all his thinking in your own home.

The developer

Built by one person. With passion.

Behind Boo. is Andreas Pfeiffer, a development engineer from Baden-Württemberg. Electronics, circuit board, firmware, housing and app all come from him – from the first draft to the finished prototype.

Since autumn 2026, Boo. has been growing step by step: from a hobby build to his own circuit board, from the first word to around 300 characters.
