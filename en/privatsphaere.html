Privacy

Your Boo. Your home. Your rules.

A device that listens needs trust. That’s why you choose where Boo. thinks: at home, in the cloud or with the AI of your choice.

Boo. Server · at home

Boo’s brain lives with you.

  • Speech recognition, language model and voice run on your own PC with a graphics card.
  • No voice recording leaves your home.
  • No subscription for the home server.
  • Its own console: memory, knowledge, voices, routines, calendar.
  • Privacy area with retention periods, deletion per person or device, and a copy of your data as a ZIP.
Cloud

No server of your own.

  • An external AI service recognises speech and generates the answer.
  • The display shows a cloud icon whenever Boo. is talking via the cloud.
  • If your home server is reachable, Boo. switches to it automatically when turned on.
  • For the production version, we’re assessing which cloud provider best meets data-protection requirements.
Your own AI · via API

Claude & co. as the brain.

  • Boo. can also be connected to Claude and other AI services.
  • You choose the language model – face, roles and character stay Boo.
  • The terms of the service you connect apply.
Boo. data centrePlanned

Dedicated servers for Boo.

  • If Boo. grows, we’ll build our own AI data centre.
  • Boo. will then think on our servers instead of a third-party provider’s.
  • This will come with a monthly subscription. The Boo. Server at home stays subscription-free.
Principles

What Boo. promises.

Listening

Only when you lift him

Boo. has no wake word and doesn’t listen all the time. He listens when you pick him up or switch on hands-free mode – visible as a bright ring.

Recording

Always announced. Never secret.

Boo. only records meetings when you want him to. He announces the recording and shows a red ring with “REC”.

Memory

You see what he knows

What Boo. remembers about you is shown in the app. Delete single items or reset everything – any time.

Honesty

He tells you he’s an AI

Boo. is an artificial intelligence and never pretends to be human. Roles are labelled as AI simulations.

Voices

No borrowed voices

Boo. speaks with synthetic voices. Real voices are only recreated with the person’s consent.

Children

Parental PIN

Content not meant for children stays hidden without the PIN.

Where we are

Boo. is a prototype.

The test-phase devices run either with the Boo. Server or via a cloud service. How data is processed in cloud mode will be set out bindingly before sale and described in the device’s privacy policy.
