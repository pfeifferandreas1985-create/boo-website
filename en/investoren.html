What makes Boo. different

Anyone can do features. Not character.

Timers and weather are standard on every voice assistant. Boo. wins where the big players can’t reach.

Character

A face and feelings.

Around 300 roles, each with their own voice and eye colour. Boo. gets to know you and builds a relationship.

Trust

Runs at home, too.

With the Boo. Server, Boo. thinks entirely on your own network – and keeps working even if a cloud service shuts down.

Independent

Any brain fits.

Own server, cloud or Claude & co. via API: Boo. isn’t tied to one language model.

More devices

Boos talk to each other.

In the Boo Network, up to eight Boos debate; for interpreting, several work together. Every extra Boo opens up new possibilities.

Controls

Lift, don’t say “Hey”.

No wake word, no listening in the background. Pick up, talk, put down – anyone gets it, from kids to grandma.

Single source

All built in-house.

Electronics, circuit board, firmware, housing, app and server – not a bought-in device with someone else’s logo.

Business model

Where we are

Not a concept. He’s up and running.

  • Working prototypes with their own main board and mood light
  • Firmware with a face, around 20 expressions, gestures and reminders
  • App for Windows, Linux and Android
  • Boo. Server for the home with speech recognition, language model and voices
  • Around 300 roles; Boo Network and interpreter in the test phase
  • A small test batch for first users is in the works
