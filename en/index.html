How it works

No wake word. No screen. Just Boo.

You handle him like an object, not a gadget. He understands what you do.

Lift

He listens.

Pick Boo. up. A bright ring and a soft tone show he’s all ears.

Ask

He answers.

Just ask away – about the weather, an idea, a story. His eyes show what he means.

Put down

He sleeps.

Lay him face down or say “Good night” – and he’s quiet instantly. While asleep, he shows the time.

What he can do

An assistant. Not a toy.

His personality and eyes make him likeable. At heart, Boo. helps you get through your day at the desk.

Conversations with character

Around 300 roles – from fitness coach to Renaissance inventor. Each with their own voice and eye colour.

Reminders that stick

Alarms and reminders by voice. Boo. rings, tells you – and keeps nudging until you respond.

Weather & clock

Weather as a little animation on the round display. While he sleeps, he shows the time and date in neon.

Lists & notes

“Put milk on the shopping list.” Five lists, notes, short voice memos – in the app, too.

Meeting notes

Boo. announces when he records, and the desktop app takes notes – with speakers, decisions and tasks. Never in secret.

Mood light

Eleven LEDs light up the whole sphere: candlelight, blue, rainbow – or to match his mood.

Workout timer

Two glowing rings, a countdown with beeps. Tap to pause, face down to finish.

Send to your phone

“Send that to my phone.” Recipes, lists or ideas land on your phone as a message.

Desk lamp

Boo. controls your WLED lamp – on, off, colour, effect. Only when you clearly mean it.

Privacy

Your Boo. Your rules.

You decide where Boo. thinks – on your own server, in the cloud or with the AI of your choice.

Boo. Server · at home

Everything stays on your network.

  • Speech recognition, language model and voice run on your own PC.
  • Conversations stay on your network.
  • View his memory, set retention periods, delete everything about a person.
Cloud

Ready to go.

  • No server of your own: an external service handles speech and answers.
  • A cloud icon on the display shows when Boo. is talking via the cloud.
  • If your home server is reachable, Boo. uses it automatically.
Your own AI · via API

Claude & co. as the brain.

  • Connect Boo. to Claude or other AI services.
  • Different language model, same Boo.: face, roles and character stay.
Boo. data centrePlanned

Dedicated servers for Boo.

  • If Boo. grows, we’ll add our own AI data centre – as a monthly subscription.
  • The Boo. Server at home stays subscription-free.
