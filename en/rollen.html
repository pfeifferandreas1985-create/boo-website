Voice

Each character has a fixed voice – women speak with women’s voices, men with men’s, with small differences in tone and pace.

Colour

Eyes and mood light take on the role’s colour. You can see at a glance who’s talking.

Child protection

Protected by PIN

Content not meant for children stays hidden without the parental PIN and can’t be sent to Boo.

All roles are AI simulations. They do not reflect real statements by the people portrayed. Boo. is an artificial intelligence – and he’ll tell you so.
