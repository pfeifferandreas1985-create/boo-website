Tech

What’s inside the sphere.

Electronics, circuit board, firmware and housing – all developed in-house. Here are the specs of the current prototype.

Front to back: face with magnetic mount, round display, ESP32-C3, motion sensor and amplifier, main board, battery holder with 18650 cell, sphere.

Data sheet

Prototype, as of autumn 2026.

Values for the small test-phase batch. Quite a bit may still change before production.

HousingSphere Ø 92 mm, height 88 mm · white translucent top, black face with magnets
DisplayRound, 1.28 inch, 240 × 240 pixels, IPS
ProcessorESP32-C3, 2.4 GHz wireless
SensorsMotion sensor (acceleration and rotation) · for lifting, shaking, tapping, putting down
AudioDigital microphone, amplifier, speaker
Mood light11 individually controllable RGB LEDs
BatteryLithium-ion, 18650 · charging via USB-C
RuntimeAround 35 hours in everyday use · calculated, not yet measured
SoftwareApp for Windows, Linux and Android · iPhone to follow
AIBoo. Server on your PC or a cloud service
One board

Everything on one round board.

The main board sits directly behind the face. Display and modules plug into the front; the battery holder, charging module and mood-light LEDs sit on the back.

Mood light

Light that shines through the shell.

The LEDs sit behind the face and shine into the sphere. The thin white shell diffuses the light until the whole sphere glows evenly.
