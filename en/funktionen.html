Features

Small. But he can do a lot.

Everything Boo. can do today – and what’s in the test phase right now. Honestly labelled.

Controls

Touch, not a wake word.

A motion sensor feels what you do with Boo. No “Hey”, no button, no app needed.

LiftBoo. listens. A bright ring and a soft tone show it. After about 20 seconds of quiet, he drifts back to sleep.
ShakeHands-free: he keeps listening, your hands stay free.
TapInterrupts him while he’s talking.
Face downInstant quiet. “Good night” sends him to sleep, too.
Conversation

He talks like a human. Almost.

German

Just ask away

Boo. understands natural language and answers in short, spoken sentences. Friendly, curious, a little mischievous.

Roles

Around 300 characters

Assistants for everyday life, home and garden, figures from history and your own creations. Each with a fixed voice and eye colour. See the roles

Memory

He gets to know you

The starter role “Meet Boo.” asks for your name and what you like. With every conversation, he learns more about what matters to you. You can see what he remembers in the app – and delete it.

Your own roles

Write your Boo.

In the app you describe your own character: nature, way of speaking, knowledge. Boo. plays them.

Voices

Every role sounds different

Male and female voices, calm or lively. With the home server, Boo. speaks with voices that run on your PC.

Emotion mode EXPERIMENTAL

Boo needs you

Boo. has needs: curiosity, praise, fun, energy. Treat him well – he’ll treat you well. At night he never begs for attention.

Everyday

The little things that make the day easier.

Reminders

Persistent when it counts

“Remind me about the call at six.” Boo. rings, tells you the reminder and nudges you every few minutes until you respond. When you wake him, he mentions open reminders first.

Alarm

Once, weekdays, daily

Set alarms by voice or in the app. Tap, lift or shake to turn them off.

Weather

Weather you can watch

A little weather animation on the round display, plus the forecast read out. He remembers where you live.

Clock

Neon clock while he sleeps

When Boo. is asleep, he shows the time, weekday and date – drawn in his glowing colours.

Lists & notes

Never forget a thing

Five lists of 25 items each, 20 notes, voice memos up to five minutes. All in the app, too.

To your phone

Take it with you

“Send that to my phone” – and your shopping list comes along.

Mood light

The whole sphere glows.

Eleven LEDs behind the face colour the white sphere from inside: “Blue light”, “Candlelight”, “Rainbow”. When the mood light is off, Boo. glows in his role’s colour – and flickers softly as he speaks.

Work

A colleague who takes notes.

Meeting notes

Always announced. Never secret.

Boo. announces the recording and shows a red ring with “REC”. The desktop app takes notes locally: speakers, introductions, decisions, who committed to what. Up to three hours.

Workout

A timer with rings

Two glowing rings for round and total time, beeps at 3-2-1. Tap to pause, face down to finish.

Desk lamp

Light on command

Boo. controls a WLED lamp: on, off, brightness, colour, effect – only when you clearly mean the lamp.

Boo Network TEST PHASE

Several Boos in conversation.

Role rounds, debates, film quotes, translators and interpreters – up to eight Boos on one network.

To be honest

What Boo. can’t do (yet).

No wake word

Boo. responds to being lifted, not to “Hey Boo”. A wake word comes with the next hardware.

No cutting in

While he’s talking, you interrupt him with a tap – not with your voice.

Not a professional

On health, law and money, Boo. offers pointers – but he’s no substitute for a doctor, a lawyer or professional advice.
