FAQ

Frequently asked.

Your question isn’t here? Ask Boo. – or drop us a line via the waiting list.

General
What is Boo.?

Boo. is a small AI voice assistant for your desk – a sphere with a round display for a face. You pick him up, ask in German and hear the answer. He has character, feelings and, if you like, around 300 different roles.

Can I buy Boo. yet?

Not yet. Boo. is a prototype. The first devices are going into a small test phase. Join the waiting list and you’ll be the first to hear what happens next.

How much does Boo. cost?

The price isn’t set yet. We’ll fix it once production is planned – and tell everyone on the waiting list first.

How is Boo. different from Alexa or Siri?

Classic voice assistants answer commands in a neutral voice. Boo. is a character: he has a face and feelings, slips into around 300 roles and becomes a specialist – from head chef to historical figure.

He gets to know you and remembers what matters to you. You use him by picking him up and putting him down instead of a wake word, several Boos can talk to each other or interpret, and you decide whether he thinks in the cloud or on your own server.

Is Boo. a toy?

No. Boo. is an assistant for adults. His personality and eyes make him likeable, but at heart he helps with everyday life and work.

Using Boo.
Do I have to say “Hey Boo”?

No. Boo. has no wake word. You lift him and he listens. For hands-free mode, give him a quick shake. Put him face down and he’s asleep instantly.

Can I interrupt him?

Yes – tap him while he’s talking. Cutting in with your voice doesn’t work on the current prototype yet.

Do I need the app?

For setup, yes: the app connects Boo. to your home network and guides you through six steps. After that, you only need it to switch roles, check lists or change settings.

Which devices does the app support?

Windows, Linux and Android. An iPhone app will follow later.

How long does the battery last?

Around 35 hours of everyday use – calculated. We haven’t measured it yet. Boo. charges via USB-C.

Privacy
Is Boo. always listening?

No. Without a wake word, he doesn’t eavesdrop in the background. He listens when you lift him or switch on hands-free mode – a bright ring shows it.

Where are my questions processed?

You decide. With the Boo. Server, speech recognition, language model and voice run on your own PC, and no voice recording leaves your home. Without a home server, Boo. uses a cloud service; the display then shows a cloud icon. You can also connect Boo. to Claude or other AI services.

If Boo. grows, we plan our own AI data centre with a monthly subscription. The Boo. Server at home stays subscription-free.

What does the Boo. Server need?

A PC with a recent graphics card on the same network. The server comes with speech recognition, a local language model and German voices.

Can I delete what Boo. knows about me?

Yes. You can see his memory in the app and delete single items or everything. The home server also offers retention periods and a copy of your data as a ZIP file.

Roles & Boo Network
Do the roles really talk like the real people?

No. Roles are AI simulations: Boo. answers the way the person might have answered, based on everything we know about them. They are not real statements.

Can I create my own characters?

Yes. In the app you describe your character’s nature, way of speaking and knowledge – and Boo. plays them.

How many Boos can talk to each other?

In the Boo Network, up to eight on the same network; for interpreting, up to three. The Boo Network is new and still in its test phase.
